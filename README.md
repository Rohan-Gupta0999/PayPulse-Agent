<h2>PayPulse-Agent</h2>
An autonomous AI teammate for retail merchants designed to monitor business metrics, detect volume and inventory anomalies, and execute human-in-the-loop revenue recovery workflows.

Overview
Merchants do not want another passive analytics dashboard; they want an operational teammate that watches the business and acts when things go wrong. PayPulse-Agent provides a unified command center combining automated anomaly detection with strict human-in-the-loop guardrails.

<h3>Core Workflows</h3>
Continuous Monitoring: Tracks business streams, transaction settlements, and operational health in the background.

Anomaly Detection: Automatically flags unexpected business slumps (e.g., Tuesday afternoon order volume dropping by 62%).

Intelligent Diagnostics: Audits underlying data layers (such as at-risk or expiring inventory portions).

Action & Verification: Formulates dynamic recovery campaigns (e.g., targeted flash combos via WhatsApp) requiring 1-click merchant approval before execution.
